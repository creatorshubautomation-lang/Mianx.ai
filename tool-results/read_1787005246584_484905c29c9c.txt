     1→# MIANX.AI CORE --- BILLING, SUBSCRIPTION & ENTITLEMENT PLATFORM SPECIFICATION v1.0
     2→
     3→**Product:** Mianx.ai\
     4→**Document:** Billing, Subscription & Entitlement Platform\
     5→**Version:** 1.0\
     6→**Status:** Architecture / Pre-Implementation\
     7→**Purpose:** Define the commercial SaaS foundation that controls plans,
     8→subscriptions, domains, modules, usage, AI consumption, limits, trials,
     9→billing lifecycle and customer entitlements.
    10→
    11→------------------------------------------------------------------------
    12→
    13→# 1. Purpose
    14→
    15→Mianx.ai must be commercially capable before individual Domain OS
    16→products scale.
    17→
    18→The billing platform therefore belongs to **Mianx.ai Core**.
    19→
    20→``` text
    21→Customer
    22→   ↓
    23→Organization
    24→   ↓
    25→Subscription
    26→   ↓
    27→Plan
    28→   ↓
    29→Entitlements
    30→   ↓
    31→Domains / Modules / Features
    32→   ↓
    33→Usage
    34→   ↓
    35→Billing
    36→```
    37→
    38→The goal is to make every domain commercially configurable without
    39→creating a separate billing system.
    40→
    41→------------------------------------------------------------------------
    42→
    43→# 2. Commercial Constitution
    44→
    45→1.  Billing is a Core platform capability.
    46→2.  Subscription status determines commercial access.
    47→3.  Authorization still determines security access.
    48→4.  Entitlement determines whether a capability is commercially enabled.
    49→5.  Feature flags are not billing.
    50→6.  Usage metering must be independent from UI display.
    51→7.  AI usage and AI cost must be measurable.
    52→8.  Domain products must be packageable independently or together.
    53→9.  Upgrade/downgrade must preserve data safely.
    54→10. Billing failures must have controlled grace and suspension behavior.
    55→

   187→# 8. Entitlements
   188→
   189→Entitlements answer:
   190→
   191→> Is this organization commercially allowed to use this capability?
   192→
   193→Possible states:
   194→
   195→``` text
   196→enabled
   197→disabled
   198→limited
   199→trial
   200→expired
   201→suspended
   202→```
   203→
   204→Authorization answers whether the user may use it.
   205→
   206→Entitlement answers whether the organization has purchased it.
   207→
   208→Both checks may be required.
   209→
   210→------------------------------------------------------------------------

   352→
   353→``` text

   355→ ↓
   356→Deduplication
   357→ ↓
   358→Accepted
   359→ ↓
   360→Aggregation

   580→# 26. Downgrade
   581→
   582→Downgrade requires special care.
   583→
   584→If the customer currently exceeds the new limits:
   585→
   586→``` text
   587→Current usage > new limit
   588→```

   590→Options may include:
   591→
   592→``` text
   593→block downgrade
   594→schedule downgrade
   595→allow downgrade but restrict new creation
   596→request cleanup
   597→```
   598→
   599→Never silently delete customer data to satisfy a lower plan.
   600→
